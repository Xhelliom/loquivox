One hotkey away

No window switching, no copy-paste gymnastics. Press a key, speak, done.

🗣️

Talk modeF6

Speech in, speech out. Discuss what you need — no key to hold — and the assistant asks who it is for, what tone, what must be in. Say the word, and the whole conversation becomes one finished text, typed and copied. Select text first and it is the text to rework.

💬

AI ChatF4

The same conversation, about what is on your screen — the error, the page, the text you selected. Nothing written at the end, unless you say “write it”. Or type straight into the overlay; pin it on top with F9.

🔎

It looks things up

Need a fact mid-sentence — a price, a date, a version, a person? The assistant hands the question to a web-searching model, says it is looking it up, and keeps talking. The answer arrives between turns and lands in the final text.

✋

Interrupt it

Talk over the reply and it stops mid-sentence and listens — your first words are kept. Turns end on meaning, not on a stopwatch: a small on-device model reads your prosody and lets you hesitate.

👁️

It can see your screen

Optionally, the focused window is captured and described once by a vision model, in parallel with your first sentence. You never have to read the stack trace out loud. Press S to look again.

🎙️

DictationF3

Speak and watch your words appear at the cursor — in any application. Backed by Whisper large-v3 for state-of-the-art accuracy, with refinement levels and live translation.

🎭

Two engines, one promise

Cascade: transcription → chat model → voice, every slot local or cloud, ~1.8 s. Realtime: one OpenAI speech-to-speech session, ~1.0 s, with the prosody of speech. Presets pick a whole setup in one click.

🔊

A voice of its ownF10

Replies are read aloud by the voice you pick: OpenAI's multilingual voices, Groq's Orpheus, or Piper, fully on your machine — no key, no network, first sample in 0.2 s.

How a turn travels

🎤 Mic→ 📝 Whispercloud or local→ 🧭 Smart Turnon-device→ 🧠 Chat model+ 🔎 research→ 🔊 VoiceOpenAI · Groq · Piper

or, with the Realtime engine: one OpenAI speech-to-speech session holds the whole conversation — ~1.0 s and the prosody of speech, the server handles turn-taking and interruption.

  • It reasons before it writes. Two sentences, one question at a time — audience, intent, tone, key facts. It never quietly guesses at a misheard name: it quotes it back and asks.
  • It searches without stopping. A research() tool hands the question to a web-searching model on a thread; the reply is spoken between turns, never mid-sentence, and stays in the brief.
  • It knows when you're done. Say “j'ai fini”, press Enter, or let the model decide. The conversation becomes one text, reviewed before it is typed — V takes you back to fix it by voice.
  • Every slot is your call. Presets Fast, Natural and Private fill transcription, engine, model and voice at once — the last one keeps the voice and the turn detection on your machine.

DictateF3

Hold the floor. Your speech lands as text at the cursor, in whatever app has focus.

Click a glowing key — or press the real one on your keyboard. Go on, this page is listening too. 😉

While dictating: Esc cancels, Space pauses & resumes, and an optional Refine key lets you pick this dictation's polish level on the fly.

In a conversation: Space ends the turn, S looks at the screen again, T sends the selection, Enter writes the text.

Get Loquivox

Pick your flavor. Every path ends with you talking to your computer.

# with an AUR helper yay -S loquivox # allow global hotkeys, then log out & back in sudo usermod -aG input $USER

Builds a static whisper-cli for offline transcription out of the box.

# download the latest .deb from the releases page, then: sudo apt install ./loquivox_*_amd64.deb # allow global hotkeys, then log out & back in sudo usermod -aG input $USER

Get the package on the releases page.

# clone and run the setup script — it detects your distro & session git clone https://github.com/Xhelliom/loquivox.git && cd loquivox ./setup.sh # allow global hotkeys, then log out & back in sudo usermod -aG input $USER # launch loquivox

Works on Debian, Ubuntu and Arch — X11 and Wayland are auto-detected.
